ACCOUNT SIZE RECOMMENDATIONS
================================================================================

Minimum Account Sizes by Risk Profile:
- Conservative: $2,000
- Moderate: $5,000
- Aggressive: $10,000
- Scalping Optimized: $3,000
- Swing Trading: $2,000

Lot Size Calculator:
Account Balance × Risk% ÷ Stop Loss in USD = Lot Size

Example:
- Account: $5,000
- Risk: 2%
- Stop Loss: 200 points = $20 per 0.01 lot
- Calculation: $5,000 × 0.02 ÷ $20 = 5 × 0.01 lot = 0.05 lots
